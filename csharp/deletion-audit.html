LinksDecoratorBase forwards calls; the automatic stack does not require a validator. Explicit existence/usages/uniqueness validators, exception suppression, logging, and transactions are selectable layers, so their named behavior is not a compulsory-core-contract violation. Rust try_get_link in an explicitly chosen helper is likewise appropriate; the issue is its unavoidable use in root writes.

Index detachment and split internal/external reclassification are necessary for the selected representation. Address-capacity limits are structural bounds. They were not removed or misreported as optional application validation. The audit does not claim an index-free backend or fully optional allocator is already available.

Verification

The three new contract tests fail against the previous PR implementation and pass after removing its mandatory checks. The baseline runner reproduces this safely with valid reset links and restores source files afterward. Experiment instructions preserve commands, finite inputs and Rust build limits. The example exercises raw, per-call, pair-based, throw, ignore, usages-reject, cascade and snapshot bulk deletion in both stores.

The updated PR records final local and CI results at its final commit. Other reports above remain separate and are not acceptance criteria to implement here, as explicitly requested by the reviewer.
