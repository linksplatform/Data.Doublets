## Scope and constraints

Historical dev/log snapshots are evidence, not live dependency manifests. They are deliberately preserved rather than rewritten to look current. C# and C++ Platform packages are distinct, independently versioned artifacts; their version numbers cannot be unified across registries. C++20 and C11 remain the public source-language contracts while the actual compilers are current. The newest published Gitpod image is frozen by its publisher; its native setup installs the current CMake and Conan independently.

Six package coordinates remain below their registries' newest semver because current upstream parents cannot use those APIs: Platform.Collections.Methods, Perfolizer, Pragmastat, platform-data, r-efi and windows-link. Their exact reasons and evidence are in the inventory and [UPSTREAM.md](UPSTREAM.md). This is the issue's explicitly permitted upstream-bug/compatibility exception, not a claim that those six versions were upgraded.

Raw CI logs are downloaded under ci-logs and ignored by the repository's existing *.log rule. Concise reproducible commands, error line references and selected validation logs are committed alongside the registry snapshots, avoiding a large duplicate of hosted CI logs. Final current-head results are also visible directly on the PR.
